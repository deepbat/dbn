01 · The person

Office hours.
Curious always.

Behind the screen: documents by day, software by evening, chai throughout.

My everyday work covers documents, finance, Excel, payments, grants, scholarships and reports - the kind of work where one wrong cell at 5pm ruins an evening. I love my work, and I like making it smoother: AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini handle the practical tasks, and I keep looking for ways to use technology to make work more fun and easier.

Outside office hours I build software with Python, Linux and APIs, and experiment with local language models, Ollama, OpenClaw, embeddings and NVIDIA CUDA. Beyond the screen: a good web series or movie, photography, family time, chai and music - usually something Punjabi playing in the background.

- Deepak, Jalandhar
